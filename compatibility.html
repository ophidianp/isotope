Compatibility

Real mods. Real results.

We took Fabric mods from Modrinth built for 26.1.2 and 26.2 and booted them on a Minecraft 26.3 dedicated server, then took 26.3 mods and booted them on 26.2. Here is exactly what happened. Every "held back" mod was stopped by Isotope before it could crash; where it says "confirmed", we switched the safety check off and watched the crash happen.

Minecraft 26.3 and 26.2 servers

Limits

What it cannot do.

Isotope is a compatibility layer, not a port. These are the walls it hits.

A

Redesigned systems

If a system was rebuilt, the old calls have no equivalent. World-generation mods crossing 26.2 → 26.3 will generally not work.

REDESIGN
B

Changed behaviour

A method that kept its name but now does something different looks fine to Isotope. Only playing shows it.

SILENT
C

Data files

Recipes, loot tables and world-gen JSON are not converted yet. The game may skip an old mod's data with errors in the log.

NOT YET
D

Older Minecraft

1.21.x and earlier are obfuscated and use a different toolchain. Out of scope.

OUT OF SCOPE
E

Clients, so far

Only tested on a dedicated server. Rendering, screens and client mixins of rewritten mods have not run in a real client yet.

UNTESTED

Other projects

Others in this space. Some may suit you better.

CLOSEST MATCH

Retromod

Rewrites old mods so they load on current Minecraft, across a much wider range of versions and loaders, and far more established. If Retromod handles your mod, use it.
